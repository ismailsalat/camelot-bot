Camelot TV Online Preview - external-source risk notice

This package makes HTTPS calls to third-party anime content providers. Their websites,
JavaScript SDK adapters, and signed media URLs are untrusted. Sources may be
unauthorized to redistribute copyrighted works and may block Railway server IPs.
Use streams you have rights to access and share. Do not circumvent DRM or access controls.

The Discord self-bot transport is unofficial and prohibited by Discord. Use a disposable
account. Streaming doesn't become authorized by using a separate account.
No credentials are embedded in this ZIP. Install only from sources you trust.
